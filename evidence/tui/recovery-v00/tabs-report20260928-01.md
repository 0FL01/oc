## Capture and timing semantics

All images frame the **entire terminal screen rectangle**, at 14 px DejaVu Sans Mono, DPR 1, Unicode 11 and the common VT parser. Cells retain every symbol, resolved foreground/background, modifier, width and cursor. Comparison invokes `tui-recovery/scripts/compare_frames.py` on complete cells and PNGs, with no field normalization, mask, content crop, resize or tolerance. Location paths, elapsed response times and unrelated independently animated content remain visible and can produce real differences.

The camera may temporarily hold **frontend VT ingestion** at an observed real byte boundary while taking that prefix's PNG. The application continues running; deferred real bytes are retained and applied afterward. There is no product SIGSTOP, injected application clock, inferred intermediate render, native-only reference, or globally synchronized spinner/marquee/scanner clock. Title phases and spinner glyphs are attested from the actual parsed grid at the capture boundary. Every comparison derives/verifies the actual tab spinner glyphs from its complete captured grid, including ordinary running/cancel-running frames; busy-title comparisons also require those genuinely observed glyphs to match. An unmatched phase is blocked honestly. This alignment covers the target tab components, not an invented common epoch for other animated content.

Default `--timeline captures` stores the complete styled grid/cursor for every captured PNG boundary in `timeline.jsonl.gz`. `observations.json`, `output-chunks.json` and `raw.vt` retain all actual observed/coalesced boundaries and all original product bytes/timestamps. Coalescing seeks distinctive VT text prefixes only to choose a real byte boundary; the complete parsed title is subsequently verified. The optional `--timeline all` decodes every retained real VT chunk after exit through the same frontend; it replaces no application clock and is substantially more expensive. Interrupted expensive attempts are retained below.

CPU is charged to the actual product process, never the browser/Python harness. Source `/proc` timestamps and all-thread measurements are retained. **Zero terminal writes is not evidence of zero application frames or scheduler wakes.** The native runner requests the existing `OC_TUI_TEST_METRICS` final file and retains it without substituting PTY-write counts for app counters. Its frame/wakeup samples must be qualified using the real owner's epoch/counters after release; the checker leaves this gate pending rather than fabricating an alignment to an unrelated clock.
