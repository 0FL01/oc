# T48 closed: owner removed the Zen scope

## Result

On 2026-09-23 the owner decided to drop OpenCode Zen from the product entirely. The provider code (`zen_catalog.rs`, `zen_chat.rs`), the actual-binary suite (`crates/oc/tests/zen_free.rs`), the wiring in config/composition/runtime/application/provider/lib and the `opencode` provider handling were removed from the working tree; `GOAL.md`, `docs/TEST_PLAN.md`, `planning/tasks.json` and `planning/acceptance.json` were restored to their pre-Zen state, and the scope document `docs/goals/2026-09-23-zen-free-chat.md` was deleted. T48 is deregistered from the live registry; this leaf, the earlier two leaves and `evidence/T48/*` remain as the historical record, and decision D18 in `docs/DECISIONS.md` supersedes D16 while keeping D17's general prohibition on client-identity mimicry.

## Checks

The earlier qualification (ZEN01/ZEN02 offline PASS, ZEN03 blocked by the provider's free-tier client gate) was never a product acceptance; its evidence is `evidence/T48/report.md` and `evidence/T48/free-tier-gate.md`. After removal the workspace is rebuilt and re-qualified with the ordinary gates (fmt, clippy, workspace tests, build, doc/journal validators) in the same commit; no Zen identifiers remain in tracked code or live planning documents.

## Risks

The journal directory and evidence for T48 stay on disk without a registry entry, so `progress.py` no longer validates them; they are historical only. Nothing in the product depends on the removed code.
